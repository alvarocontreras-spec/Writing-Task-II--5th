WRITING TASK: WHAT WE WEAR

5th grade · Look, write, check and download.

🎯 Your mission

Choose one person in the park picture. Write three short sentences about what a person is doing and wearing right now.

Ayuda: Elige una de las siete personas del parque. Escribe una oración en cada casilla. Usa is + verbo terminado en -ing (por ejemplo, She is riding). Al menos una oración debe describir la ropa con is wearing. Revisa los comentarios y corrige antes de descargar.

Time: About 10 minutes. You can use your book and notes.

Find the seven people from left to right across the park.

1. Bicycle2. Football3. Dog4. Book5. Rain6. Ice cream7. Photo

💡 Three ideas

1. What is the person doing?

2. What is the person wearing?

3. What else can you see the person doing or wearing?

🧱 Useful language

The girl is riding / walking / jumping.

The boy is playing / eating.

He / She is wearing a jacket / jeans / a T-shirt.

Do not copy this example: The cat is reading a newspaper.
